# Why Forward Deployed Engineering?

The part of engineering I enjoy most is the point where technology meets a messy real-world problem.

I like to:

1. understand the user or business problem,
2. reduce ambiguity,
3. prototype quickly,
4. choose an architecture that can survive beyond the prototype,
5. implement the critical parts,
6. validate the system,
7. ship it,
8. learn from actual usage.

That is why FDE is a natural direction for me. I do not want to stop at a model, an API, or a slide deck. I want to help turn the whole problem into a working outcome.
